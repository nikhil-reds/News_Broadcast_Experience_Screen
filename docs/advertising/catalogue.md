# Advertising catalogue

The shared catalogue is defined in lib/ad-catalog-data.json and exposed by
lib/ad-catalog.ts. It contains 21 PNG creatives and six brands:

| Industry | Brands |
| --- | --- |
| FMCG | Aashirvaad Shudh Chakki Atta; Dove Body Wash |
| Consumer Electronics | Samsung Galaxy Smartphone; Sony Wireless Headphones |
| Banking & Finance | Axis Bank Credit Card; HDFC Bank Credit Card |

Assets live under public/ads/industries/<industry>/<brand>/.
The former public/ads/industry and templates directories have been removed.
