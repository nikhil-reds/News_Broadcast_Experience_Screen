# Artwork layout conventions

Each creative declares a layout: l-band, bottom, side, or side-frame.
adVideoBounds in lib/ad-catalog.ts reserves the programme window.

- Bottom and L-band creatives reserve 19.5% at the bottom.
- Side and L-band creatives normally reserve 13.6% at the left.
- The wide Aashirvaad side-frame variant reserves 43.2% at the left.
- Narrow side images are placed in the left strip, not stretched over the screen.

The supplied image named Dove inside the Aashirvaad source directory actually
shows Aashirvaad artwork and is assigned accordingly. Original transparency
and resolution are retained.
